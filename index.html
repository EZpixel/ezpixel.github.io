Blunday

Get Rich or Fall Trying

Blunday is a small blue cube with big dreams. Bounce from platform to platform, grab every coin, gem and gold bar in sight, and climb as high as you dare. One wrong jump and it's a long way down.

Spend your riches on upgrades, dress Blunday up in the wardrobe, and chase achievements and high scores with your friends on Google Play Games.

Play in your browser
  • 🚀 Power-upsJetpacks, spring boots, umbrellas, magnets and stars.
  • 💰 UpgradesTurn your gold into higher jumps, more fuel and a Ctrl+Z Jetpack.
  • 🎩 WardrobeHats, glasses, colours and emotes, because a cube has feelings too.
  • 🏆 Google Play GamesAchievements, a high-score leaderboard and cloud saves.
